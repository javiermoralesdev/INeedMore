I Need More is a collection of 5 arcade minigames with simple one-touch controls.

- FlapSpace: Navigate through obstacles in this fast-paced flapper
- SpaceRun: Run as far as you can while dodging everything in your path
- Snake: The classic game. Eat, grow, don't bite yourself
- SkyRise: Jump your way to the top and don't fall
- FinalBattle: You're surrounded. Shoot everything that moves

Unlock all minigames in Story Mode, then play them endlessly in Free Mode and chase your high scores.

100% free and open source under the GPL 3.
